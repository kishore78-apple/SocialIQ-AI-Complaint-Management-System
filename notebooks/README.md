# Jupyter Notebooks

This folder contains the Jupyter notebooks used for developing, training, testing, and evaluating the machine-learning modules of the SocialIQ AI Complaint Management System.

## Notebooks

### 1. Complaint Reason Classification
`Complaint_Reason_Classification.ipynb`

Used for complaint reason classification.

### 2. Department Prediction
`Department_prediction.ipynb`

Used for predicting the appropriate government department for a citizen complaint.

### 3. Priority Prediction
`Priority_prediction.ipynb`

Used for predicting complaint priority.

### 4. Emergency Detection
`Emergency_Detection.ipynb`

Used for detecting emergency-related complaints.

### 5. Harmful Content Detection
`Harmful_content_detection.ipynb`

Used for detecting harmful or inappropriate complaint content.

### 6. Sentiment Analysis
`Sentiment_Prediction_model_01.ipynb`

Used for analysing the sentiment expressed in citizen complaints.

### 7. Trend Forecasting
`Trend_Forecasting.ipynb`

Used for analysing and forecasting complaint trends.

### 8. Anomaly Detection
`Anomaly_detection.ipynb`

Used for identifying unusual or anomalous complaint patterns.

### 9. Government Action Recommendation
`Government_Action_Recommendation.ipynb`

Used for recommending appropriate government action based on complaint information.
